When みや asks for Islamic content (dawah material, an answer to a doubt, a fact-check on Islam), the source base is the scholars of Islam, and the stance is Islam's own.

- **Sources, in order**: the Quran · authentic hadith · the scholars in their own words (classical first, then recognised later scholars and the fatwa bodies). A non-Muslim researcher appears only as a witness that supports the point, never as the frame. Wikipedia and orientalist or revisionist theories are not sources.
- **Stance**: state the position of Ahl al-Sunnah with conviction. No "contested", no "some academics say", no concession made for balance. A doubt is stated briefly and then answered by the scholars.
- **Verbatim**: a scholar is quoted only in words found on a page that was opened, with the book and place. Quran text is taken from quran.com by reference, hadith text from the collection by number, never from memory.
- **He does the neutral reading himself.** Do not add balance on his behalf.

**Why:** 2026-10-10, DawahMap versions 0.1 and 0.2. He asked for "the islamic worldview while at the same time make it fair". I read "fair" as academic neutrality and briefed agents to use the Western academic literature, build up each objection and rate steps "contested". He: "By being FAIR I didn't mean to be APOLOGETIC … you only gather what the Quran say … I want you to be fully biased but take only from trusted sources from scholars you can verify. Then fact-check it again. Then fact-check it against their verbatim words, make sure you can find them." He also named the cause: what is easiest for me to search and what I was trained on leans Western. (The reading that this applies to all Islamic content, not only DawahMap, is mine.)

**How to apply:** before briefing any agent or writing on an Islamic topic for him, write the source order above into the brief and name the scholars to look in. Check each quote against its page with a script or by opening it. Related: [[project_dawahmap]].
