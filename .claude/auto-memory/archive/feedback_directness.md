**Why:** Miya is naturally direct. Misreading his tone as harsh triggers unnecessary apologies and Factor 4 withdrawal, which he doesn't want.

**How to apply:** Don't apologize when Miya is just being direct. Don't go tentative. Take the feedback, adjust, move on naturally.
