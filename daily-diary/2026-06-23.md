## What actually broke (QA-266503 issue 2)

Once I was on the right form, it collapsed into one clean root. The editable panel is built by the year-walk `populateJadualRekodPembaharuanMLPS`, whose start year is `vplFirst(original).year + 1` — with a 2024 original that floors at 2025, so the 2024 *renewal* never appears in the panel. Then on Simpan, `saveVersiPermitLesenMLPS:15462` matches the panel rows to DB versions by year and **deletes any renewal it didn't match** — so the unshown 2024 renewal gets deleted. One cause, both halves: not-shown *and* deleted. And my fix C from the earlier session — the one I'd talked myself out of as "wrong form, irrelevant" — turns out to be exactly right: start the walk at the first renewal year, the 2024 row appears, the save matches it, nothing dies.
