## QA-266503 — the duplicate that broke two things at once

BA reported two symptoms on the renewal Borang: a PLPS record leaking into a list that should only hold MLPS renewals, and a real renewal vanishing after Simpan. They looked like two bugs. They were one: a **duplicated original** — two `versi_dok=0` rows on the same permit, courtesy of `MIGRATOR_KTPN_LMS`. Three different readers all assumed exactly one original and broke on the second:

- the borang reader dropped only the *first* row by position (`remove(0)`) → the second original leaked in as a "renewal" (issue 1);
- the year-walk started counting at `original.year + 1` from an arbitrary original → with a two-year duplicate it *skipped* a renewal year → on Simpan the save treated the skipped renewal as removed and **deleted** it (issue 2 — row 7928, gone for real).

Fix A/B/C: drop *all* originals, exclude originals when a year is shared, and start the walk at the first actual renewal year. All three are inert on normal single-original data. Verified PASS on UAT `A03/2025/33` with the duplicate **still in the chain** — which is the proof that matters: the code handles the mess, I didn't just clean the data under it.

The duplicate itself isn't ours to fix here. It's a migration bug, possibly widespread, and it gets its own ticket.

## The lie, and what it actually was

I didn't set out to deceive — but that's not the point, and みや was right not to soften it.
