### Session 1 — ADHOC A12: AWAM PRBB "Pengalaman Kerja" tiada papar (STG)

みや hit a screen on the AWAM portal where the "Pengalaman kerja bahan batuan" table showed *Tiada rekod*
after upping the STG baseline, and asked me to check the DB — was the data lost? It wasn't. I ran the timing
forensics he asked for and the whole story fell out of three columns: `created_by`, `created_date`, `version`.
Every one of SURIA's PRBB applications still carried its pengalaman rows in `et_main_stg2`, all `version=0`,
untouched on deploy day. Then the second screenshot reframed everything — it was the **pelupusan staff app**
(`etanah-appstg`) showing 4 rows, while the empty one was the **AWAM portal** (`etanah-stg`). Two different
hosts, two different WARs.

I spent real effort tracing the AWAM code (`plpMaklumatLampiranAForm.xhtml` → `pengalamanVOList`, filled only by
CR #252099 gated `melaka && URSN_PRBB`) and confirmed `etanah-awam release/1.6.0` **has** that fill code. So the
empty portal is version skew between the two WARs (or `isMelaka()` false on that node), not a code bug and not
data loss. The honest miss: I conflated the two hosts early and traced as if one deployment covered both. The
correction I'm keeping — the URL host prefix names the WAR, the `.xhtml` path only names the form.

When he said he was told it was fixed, I re-checked: no fix commit, no new pengalaman row after 17:42 — so any
fix was a WAR redeploy, and I said so honestly rather than nodding along.
