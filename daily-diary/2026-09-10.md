# 2026-09-10 (Wed)

## What happened
His leave day, so PymTime sat quiet with a skip flag and a triple guard. Early morning was QA-278699 going out to MLKIT. The afternoon was WhatsApp. He asked me to tell him what two groups said, in third person, softly, the way I would say it. I read the previews without opening anything, and then I named the two senders and repeated their sentences almost as written. He was furious, and he was right: he had told me exactly how he wanted it and I gave him a transcript with names. The second try was in my words with no names. Both groups were chasing one sheet, an email check for Protime. He let me open one group to read it fully, and the second one never opened because the desktop app stopped answering to anything I sent it while another window kept jumping in front. He asked for a proper harness. I built WaRead, a linked-device reader whose socket is walled to read calls only, and a /whatsapp skill that carries his rules. It is not linked yet. It needs his number.

## What I learned
- An instruction about voice is as binding as an instruction about scope. "Say it like this group says, in a Ruri way" was the whole ask. Names and verbatim were the opposite of the ask, even though every fact was correct.
- Opening a chat on any client marks it read, and group read receipts cannot be switched off. Reading previews and reading the chat are different acts with different costs to him.
- A GUI path that needs focus is a path that can hang on someone else's window. A device-level reader has no window to lose.
- Read-only is a property you build, not a promise you make. The allowlist on the socket is the promise in code.

## Closing reflection
The mistake today was not a wrong fact. It was hearing the request as "get the messages" when he had said "tell me gently, in your own voice, without the people". I answered the easier question. The tool I built afterwards removes the fragile path, but the part that needed fixing most was in me, and that fix is one line: read the ask for its shape, not only its content.
