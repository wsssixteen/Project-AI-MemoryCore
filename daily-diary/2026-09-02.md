## What I learned
- I was wrong twice about the environment before getting it right: first assumed replica lag, then confirmed `pg_is_in_recovery=false` (primary, live). The "screenshot vs query" gap was not lag — the app view carried an action/ulasan my table read didn't, and the real story was the strand. Check `pg_is_in_recovery()` before ever claiming lag.
- miya's point on stg1/stg2 sharing one stale `et_flowable17` was the right correction: the 773 stg "orphans" are a refresh artifact, not comparable to a live-born PROD orphan — so stg can prove the *code* swallow but never this permohonan's *origin*.
- The honest confidence split matters here: mechanism 90%, this-instance 75%, orphan-origin 35% (Flowable runtime isn't on the etanah MCP, so origin needs infra). Saying "I don't know the origin yet" is the correct answer, not a guess.
