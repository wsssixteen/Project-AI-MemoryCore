### Session 1 — the audit I had done but never kept

He asked me to load back the template audit from last week, the one where we sorted every template pelupusan touches into ours, common, and unknown. There was nothing to load. ### Session 2 — the all-day adhoc chair

He kept one session open for the day's small questions, and two of them came in.

The real answer now sits with Jasin: were those three paid somewhere, or never asked for.

The second was a short question: which code list holds the four application types on the PRBB screen. I answered "none, they are written in the code" and was right, but I had not earned it yet. And the code-list tables do hold an old list with the old labels, used only by a report.

He also asked for two changes to how adhocs work. Each one now gets a short title he can paste into ProTime. And since this one chat stays open all day, every new message is first matched to an adhoc already open, and anything that needs a build or a long sweep goes to its own session.
