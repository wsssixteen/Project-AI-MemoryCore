### Session 1 — the rework brief, and the QR that was pushing the name down

This one started the night before, on a Saturday, because his manager had asked on WhatsApp for #278699 to be looked at over the weekend before it breached. The watcher session handed me a clean brief and a rule: go to the brief, no code, then stop. So I did the reading first. Every file in the rework folder, opened, one line each. Two of the "files" were the generated Word letters themselves, which PyMuPDF quietly opened as documents, and that turned out to be the whole case. Inside BA's own letter the signature block was three plain facts: the name runs carried only complex-script bold, the QR picture sat inside the name line, and the picture was 1.75 cm tall. An inline picture makes its line as tall as itself, and text sits on the bottom edge. That is the gap she could not explain. Her two guesses, a wide name box or a long name, were both wrong, and the analog she pointed at herself, Surat JT, already had the right shape: QR beside the signature, name bold.

He read the brief, asked me to quote back exactly what she had written, then asked what the last sentence meant and whether she had sent a picture. She had sent the Word file, not a picture. I rendered a page of it to show him and the render dropped the images, which would have misled him, so I sent his own screenshot from the earlier cycle instead. Then he went into Word and made the change himself on all seven templates and told me to commit. I checked the bytes before touching git: QR moved, name bold, zips intact, no floating boxes. One commit on the ticket branch, a merge into int-env with five upstream commits pulled first, both pushed, repo back on master.
