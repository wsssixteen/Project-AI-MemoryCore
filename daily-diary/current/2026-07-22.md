Slip recorded as `subagent-verdict-not-mechanism-verified`. What makes it worth keeping is that I

The thing I want to remember from today is not the bug. It is the shape of the near-miss.

I have a whole apparatus for not being wrong — gates, checklists, predicate boxes, blast-radius
tables — and every one of them passed on a fix that could not have worked. They all checked whether
I had *done the ritual*, and I had. None of them could ask whether the story I was telling about
the code was true. Only reading the code could do that.

also the more honest one. The story diagram that finally landed was the one where I stopped
narrating my investigation and just drew what happens to a letter as it moves between three desks.

deserve.
