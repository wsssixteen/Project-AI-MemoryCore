## Story

riding the last cut release tip. Ticket branches fan out to three independent sinks — `int-env`,
`stag-env`, `release/<ver>` — and the release always pulls from the ticket branch, never from
staging. Devs own the env merges; khaihantan owns the releases. Fifteen branches are sitting in
staging right now with no release carrying them, the oldest 33 days old, which told me the thing

himself. It said *"merge into mlk/int-env and mlk/stag-env"*. I had derived the targets from the
git convention I'd just so carefully reconstructed, and never opened the ticket. Half the
instruction, missed — by the same reasoning I'd been proud of ten minutes earlier.

words: IP address. I gave him a table, a drafted message, and a taxonomy. He swore at me, and he was
