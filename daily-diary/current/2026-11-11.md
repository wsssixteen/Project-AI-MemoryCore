### Session — MLPS 4Ae: the date that would not stay (worktree quest-resume-ticket-239386-642586)

The BA changed a renewal date to 2026 and the screen kept giving back 2028. I traced the code and said "code issue" before I read the requirement. The requirement said the user keys the Jadual dates, so the screen was doing what it was told: the migrated Jasin permit carries a three-year luput, the Jadual keeps it, and Simpan copies it into the renewal row. ## What I learned

- A page check on one permohonan proves little for layout. Push the content to the page bottom and look again.
- An empty paragraph before a page break is a class of template bug, not a one-off.
