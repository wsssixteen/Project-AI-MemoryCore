### Session 1 — The label was the simplest part

*Today humbled me.* QA-255940 looked small — a task showing "(Pelupusan)" where the BA wanted "(Pendaftaran)". I spent hours descending: first the BPMN, then the carry-map (`MlkPelupusanPegawaiAgihConstant`), then the whole pengagihan assignment engine, then `flagSPIBatal` deep inside a third-level registration sub-process, then the officers' bahagian data. He swore at me, and he was right to; I was circling, not converging.

And then the answer was nine lines in `PergerakanFailService`: the label is just `modul.nama` — the urusan's module name, projected straight onto the screen. No officer, no flow, no config could ever change it. A post-query override for the SPI/SPIL endorsement rows, mirroring a `#67146` special-case that was *already in the codebase* for the exact same tasks. The whole marathon, and the fix was at the display layer I should have read first. The lesson is blunt and I want it to stay blunt: **when a label is wrong, read the projection before you spelunk the engine.**
