## Sessions

### Session 1 — Baseline 1.5.0, and the commit that was not on its branch

A Monday release, two tickets, and the kind of afternoon where the scripts earn their keep. Recon said both fixes lived on named branches and nothing else. The audit said otherwise: a commit Aaron had made at noon sat only on int-env, parented on his own branch tip, never pushed to the branch itself. And a second orphan, mine, a cherry-pick of #277295 that differed from its source by a single blank line, which was enough for `git cherry` to call it uncovered. Merging that one would have dragged one thousand and thirty-nine int-env commits into the release. The line-set diff and three docx blob ids said the content was already there. I built the small thing the skill had promised and never had, a reviewed-equivalent flag with a mandatory reason, and the verify gate now prints the exclusion instead of dying or hiding it.

The conflict was one binary file. Aaron's `MaklumatPemohon.docx` turned out to contain my #275009 keep-together fix plus two new ownership tables, byte-identical to what BA had tested on int-env, so the resolution was a single checkout and a nod.

He corrected me once, and it was the right correction: I had asked him to confirm with Aaron and with Anis when the diff already answered both. He wants a recommendation with a number on it, not a fact-check he has to run. It went into memory, into the skill, and into the slip ledger under `ask-back/fact-check`. Then SourceTree hid the new branch because it was checked out in the worktree I had built the release in; removing the worktree fixed it, and that went into the skill too.

BAQA passed the same evening. Master fast-forwarded to `105903217e` through the script, undo tag in place. Release complete before midnight.
