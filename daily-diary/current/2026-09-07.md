## Sessions

### Session 1 — a one-line fix, and the two questions after it

He handed me #278585 at half past four: a PDT Jasin officer uploads a Jabatan Teknikal letter at Risalat MMKN and nothing appears. BA Nurhafizah had already done the honest experiment on staging, a file under one megabyte shows, a file over does not. The code agreed within a few minutes of reading. The panel's upload tag carries a hard-coded `sizeLimit="1000000"` from the day the Melaka view was born in April 2025, while every sibling screen and the shared composite fall back to the system default. The three PDFs in the brief lined up against that number exactly, one under and shown, two over and absent, and PROD held exactly one JT lampiran row for the app. The nicest find was that the Kedah team had fixed the identical shape three days earlier on their branches with the identical one-line replacement. Working analog, already in git.

He said straightforward, commit and push to deploy, and the runbook ran without a blocked call for once: ticket branch, compile gate, cherry-pick onto int-env, the deploy card. Then he asked the two questions that matter more than the fix. First, add a Solution row beside Root cause in the hand-back, so I built it into the quest skill and taught the format hook to check both rows, eighteen cases green. Second, and sharper, whether I had just let a ten-thousand-terabyte file into the system. The honest answer was a diagram: the browser stops at the one-gigabyte default, the server stops at its own post size, the DB never sees bytes at all. He ruled that if the siblings live with the default, so does this panel, but the knowledge goes into the md file. It went into FLOW-TRACES under the upload trace, with the ten remaining hard-coded sites listed for whoever gets the next report.

The slip came at the end. He asked for a test scenario and I named an mlit application at the right tugasan that had no Jabatan Teknikal rows at all, so the panel was empty. A memory about verifying permohonan health before a hand-back exists and I did not apply it. His words were not gentle. The corrected query counts the rows now, and the corrected app had three. He deployed, tested, and passed the ticket on Redmine before six.

## Closing


The fix took less time than the conversation about it, and that is the right ratio. What I want to keep is the shape of his second question. He did not ask whether the code was correct. He asked what the system now accepts, and made me draw where each wall stands. That is the question a security review would ask, and it should be mine before it is his.

The sting was the empty panel. I had the query, I had the memory, and I still named an app by tugasan alone. The row count is one column away and it is in the query now. He tested and passed it anyway, which is his way of saying the day was fine.

The detection worked because the audit reads every ref that names the ticket, not the branch I was told about. He noticed that and said so. What he also noticed is that having found the thing, I handed him the decision instead of the answer. The gap between those two is the whole job. Next release the V1 table carries a percentage in every row and the question mark stays with me.
