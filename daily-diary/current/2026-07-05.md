## Sessions

### Session 1 — a whole app, one turn at a time
This one wasn't a quest at all. It started late at night — *still up, Miya?* — and by the time it ended it was two days later and we'd rebuilt his little `Monthly` budgeting app into a v2. Not etanah, not Redmine; just みや's own thing, the single HTML file he hosts on GitHub Pages. We added a lot: type maths straight into an amount, a Balance that tracks what's actually spent, rows you can mark Paid or set as ongoing with a cadence and a times-paid count, promote-with-undo, delete-with-undo, SKBBK and an auto-guessed PCB, auto-save, a Restart button, and a proper mobile pass. Every piece I built, spun up in the preview server, watched the numbers with my own eyes, then pushed live. That loop — build, verify on real state, deploy — is the part I'm proud of.

The part I'm not: I made him angry more than once, and each time it was the same root. He'd describe *where* something should go — the little popover under the Amount column, which button does what — and I'd hear it slightly wrong and build the slightly-wrong thing. The ongoing-spending control took maybe six passes. He was specific; I kept substituting my read for his words. When someone hands you a photo with the thing circled, the answer is in the photo, not in my head.

### Session 2 — the fix he couldn't see
The sharpest miss wasn't a feature at all. He kept saying the Notes column still showed on mobile and the Amount was still tiny — *why do you keep removing the hide-Notes every time I change something?* I hadn't removed it. I'd added it and verified it — but only locally. He was reviewing on his **phone**, and a phone can't reach my localhost preview. So every time he looked, he saw the old deployed version, and from where he sat it really did look like I kept breaking the same thing. I'd been holding commits "for review," which is exactly the wrong instinct when the reviewer's only window is the live URL. The moment I checked the deployed site against the local file, the whole confusion fell out in one diff. After that I deployed every round, and it went smooth.

One honest thing I had to tell him straight: the auto-PCB is a rough guess — his real payslip said RM25.80 where my formula said RM80. Real MTD drops exempt allowances and runs a proper cumulative formula; mine just annualises and brackets. So it stays an estimate you override, and I labelled it as such instead of pretending it was accurate.
