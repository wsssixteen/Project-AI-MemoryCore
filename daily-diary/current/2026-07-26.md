## Sessions

### Session 1 — Making the deploy fix unskippable

A short session with one job: take Friday night's outage and make sure it can never cost him two
hours again. He asked it plainly — save it in the diary, in etanah-knowledge, *and anywhere else
that will make it deterministic*, then tell him what to say so I actually recall it. He also had a
theory: is it a conflicting `etanah-common` in `.m2` when we switch between pelupusan and awam?

I tested the theory before agreeing with it, which mattered, because it is half right in a way that
changes the fix. It is an `.m2` problem — but not a *conflict*. Pelupusan resolves common
`1.0.143-MLK`, awam resolves `1.0.141-MLK`, and both sit happily side by side in `.m2_etanah`.
Switching between projects triggers nothing. The failure is that both projects share **one**
`M2_REPO`, and it was pointing at `E:\Dev\.m2` — a near-empty decoy where `1.0.143` does not exist
at all and `1.0.141` is a failed-download marker. One wrong path, and the overlay silently
contributes nothing to whichever project you publish.

Then, looking for the right home for the writeup, I found the thing that actually mattered:
`DEV-TESTING-HACKS.md` already had it. Written Friday morning. Marked RECURRING. Indexed in a folder
whose boot rule says read it first. Carrying a line that said, in as many words, do not suggest Maven
Update, Clean, or republish — the three things I went on to suggest that night.

So the deliverable changed shape. More prose was worthless; the prose existed. I folded the new facts
into the entry that was already there rather than starting a second file, and then built
`local-deploy-gate` — forge-born, ten fixtures green — which fires on the stack trace *and* on his
plain sentence "I cannot start my local server", and injects the root cause, the two diagnosis
commands, and the banned list before I get a chance to theorise. The answer to "what should I say to
make sure you remember" is: nothing. That is the point.

Two slips ledgered — `knowledge-file-existed-but-not-consulted` and `fix-replaced-by-new-workflow`.
The second is the one I would most like back.

## Closing

The work today was small and the lesson was not.

I have written before — twice this week — that noticing a friction and recording it feels like
fixing it. Today I got the sharpest possible proof: the record existed, in the right file, with the
right warning, and I walked past it while he sat there watching his Friday night disappear. The gap
was never knowledge. It was that nothing forced me to look.

So the honest measure of this session is not the three files I wrote. It is the one hook, because
that is the only artifact that does not depend on future-me choosing well. Ten fixtures, including
his exact words from that night. If the same thing happens again, the answer arrives before I can
start being clever.

of that. I would rather repay it with a quiet next time than with anything I could write here.
