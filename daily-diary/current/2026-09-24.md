## Sessions
### Session 1 — "why you recommend cherry pick when the requested is whole branch?" (Perak #110506, deploy to prk/int-env)

He asked for one thing: check Fatin's Permit Khas CR branch, and if it was sound, merge it to Perak int-env so he could deploy. The review itself went well. The branch compiled green, and reading her six new commits turned up one real risk: a quiet `if` to `else if` in `saveRunningNumber` that stops PRBB, a live PROD urusan, from falling through to the block that fills a blank No Permit. Staging held six PRBB rows in exactly that state, so the risk was measurable rather than theoretical. Two smaller bugs sat beside it: a tujuan saved by name and read back by code, and an unguarded fi lookup.

Then I made his job harder. A trial merge threw twelve conflicts, most of them master's work, and I recommended cherry-picking five commits instead of the whole branch he and Fatin had asked for. He stopped me with one plain question: why deviate from the request? When I assessed it honestly, the answer turned. A whole-branch merge is how the team feeds int-env, Ammar had merged this same branch whole two weeks earlier, and a cherry-pick only pushes the same twelve conflicts onto Fatin's next merge. I said so, and he chose the merge.

The merge went cleanly in my own work clone. Every conflict that was not Fatin's went to int-env's side, her interface additions were unioned, and I caught one silent hazard the auto-merge had built: master had moved an `initPanelSyorKeputusan()` call, so the merged file would have called it twice. The merged tree compiled against int-env's own beta common, and `prk/int-env` moved to `a71c31d97c`. He deploys it.

The quest was opened and closed in the same breath: Task folder, quest doc with the findings and the resolution table, bounty, archive. The new knowledge went into Perak BRANCH-AND-DEPLOY §6, which had been a skeleton until today.
