## Sessions

### Session 1 — Masterline: four looks, one switch (delegated)

The orchestrator handed me his old ruling from 2026-10-20: try every look, 1, 2, 3, a mix, or a switch. The card had waited since then, so I started straight away and did not stop to ask, because that is his rule for this project.

The work was mostly a split. The old prototype carried its look inside every rule of one stylesheet. I pulled the look out into tokens, a neutral base that knows only structure, and wrote four token sets on top: the console, the ledger, the board, and my mix of the three. The app itself barely moved: a switch, two address parameters, a greeting that became "Due work", forty-two dot-separated lines turned into commas.

Then the proving. Forty flows per look at desk and phone, fifteen thousand clicks, the detector over every screen in every layout. The detector kept finding small true things the first pass hid: the Reminders page repeated one 8-pixel step through seventeen inline styles, the report page did the same, look 2 on a phone spent a whole screen on five numbers. The critique agent found the one I liked least: gold meant both "press here" and "due this week". Now it means only the second.

The bad moment was mine. My harness made a fresh browser profile for every run and never removed one. A hundred and forty-two of them filled drive C: to nothing, and Edge stopped starting. I found it only because a slips write failed with "disk full". Deleted them, taught the harness to clean up, logged it as a blocker in `core/slips.js` (category `blocker`, two rows). A test tool can damage the machine it tests on; it needs a budget like anything else that runs.
