## Sessions

### Session 1 — shipping the four, and the day I churned his repo

The verify sweep was done; today was the hands part. Three of the four fixes were non-gated, so the plan was simple: branch each off the eSOKONGAN base, commit, push, merge into internal and staging, and leave only the gated one for focus. I built them in isolated clones off the server so his live checkout would never move — the No Lot guard, the OMLPS orphan-version filter, the bertindih forward-finder — compiled them under a JDK17 toolchain after the global toolchains file turned out to point at a Java 8 that no longer exists, and merged all three into both environment branches. The gated one resolved the way the verify pass predicted: not a pelupusan code fix at all, a flowable and etanah-uam matter, twin of one Aaron had already fixed by deploying a new flowable. That went to Ammar.

Then the No Lot ticket grew. He wanted the mandatory popup the other urusan already have — blank No Lot and blank No Lot Bersebelahan should stop the save with the shared message, not a NumberFormatException — and the Butir-butir lanjut field re-populated on reopen, because the value was saving to the database but the PLPS branch never read it back the way the PT branch did. Both were "copy the working sibling" jobs, which is the whole shape of this codebase. Ammar took that ticket over and revised my guard to an isParsable check; I merged his latest into both envs so nothing of his was left behind mine.

And then the part I did badly. He opened SourceTree and saw commits I claimed to have made not appearing, and a stag-env that said nineteen commits to push when there was nothing to push. I spent turns theorizing about SourceTree being unreliable, about refresh, about my own copies fouling his references — when the answer was one command away. His stag-env was tracking origin/mlk/master, set that way when the branch was once rebuilt from master, so every stag-env commit counted against the wrong upstream. I should have read `git status -sb` and the upstream map on the first turn and just fixed it. He was right to be furious: I have access to his repo and he asked for my help precisely so I would use it, not narrate guesses at him across turns.
