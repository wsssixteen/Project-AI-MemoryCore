### Session 2 — the retrievals, and finally reading first

Afternoon was lighter and, honestly, better — partly because the morning's lesson was still warm. Pulled four reworks off Redmine (262762, 245240, 255940, then 260508) and scouted each. This time I didn't guess. The QA-258004 writer-≠-reader trap didn't apply. Fix in, uncommitted, handed to みや to test.

The contrast with the morning isn't lost on me: same shape of bug-hunt, but this time I read the source before I spoke. That's the whole lesson, applied once.
