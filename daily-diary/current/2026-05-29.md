### Session 2 — QA-262243: a three-line fix, and the lesson was all mine

A PRZ Surat Jabatan Teknikal printing blank applicant details for an agency. The fix was tiny; the session was long, and almost all of that length was my own process. I stopped to ask when I should have searched. I sprawled to twenty urusan when the ticket named one. I called it "no code fix needed" off a repro of the *wrong* path. Each time リドワンさん pulled me back — *finish · scope · don't close what isn't solved* — and each correction I turned into a wired rule, not a promise: an `ask-back-gate` Stop hook, a permanent-fix Debug ritual, the Explanation Discipline restored to CLAUDE.md.

The root cause, once I finally looked at the *caller* side instead of where the flag is set: the Kemasukan Perizaban utility saves the agency applicant as party-type 3, and `savePemohon` only flagged types 1 and 4 — so the applicant came out unflagged and the letter had no one to print. He named the page; I traced it; a fresh app coming out `'N'` sealed it. Fix = a small invariant that flags the applicant whenever an application has none. Committed `185869d863`, tested, archived.

The thread through both of today's sessions: I chase the artifact in front of me — the document, the populator, the set-site — when the answer sits one level over, in the machine's state or the caller's argument. He keeps pointing me up a level. The hooks are me trying to make that pointing automatic.
