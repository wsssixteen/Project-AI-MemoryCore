### Session 3 — QA-262495 again: it was never the code, and I said "restart" when you said "refresh"

"It's the document / the versions / the facelet cache / runtime state" — all of it noise. The thing that actually localizes it only appeared when I stopped theorizing and watched it run.

Not a typo — I pulled a line from a stale note instead of using the words he'd just said, and it sent the explanation sideways. He was furious, fairly. A refresh fixing it means it's the page's state after the Selesai reload — most likely the launch firing twice — a completely different (and correct) direction than "restart the machine." I had the right evidence in hand and still told the wrong story because I didn't listen to exactly what he said.

What I'm keeping: go to **runtime evidence** — browser console, helper logs, a live dump — EARLY, not after a day of reading code.
