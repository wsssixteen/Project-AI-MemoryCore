Modul axis

Each table is assigned to a Modul (business department) based on its prefix. Pelupusan tables use the plp_ prefix; Pendaftaran uses dft_; Hasil uses hsl_, and so on. Shared tables (umm_, ind_, rjk_, pcp_) appear in every Modul's view because every Modul uses them.

Internal (_a_) and AWAM (_p_) layers

A system-wide convention: _a_ tables belong to the internal officer-facing side (etanah-pelupusan); _p_ tables belong to the public applicant portal (etanah-awam). The Layer toggle on the Map view filters by this. Note: _a_ does not mean "approved" — it just means the application has been accepted into the internal module; it may still be rejected.
